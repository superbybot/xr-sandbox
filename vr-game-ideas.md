# VR Game Ideas Brainstorm

## Standalone Demo Concepts (Mechanic Showcases)

- **Gravity Lab** — Physics sandbox with objects of distinct materials (foam, metal, squishy). Demonstrates haptic differences per material on grab/throw.
- **Spatial Inventory System** — RPG-style 3D inventory where items float and menus expand contextually. Shows spatial UI design vs. flat UI in VR.
- **Multi-Surface Locomotion Demo** — Compact level using teleport, smooth movement, and climbing. Each zone demonstrates when and how to use each locomotion type.
- **Tool-Based Interaction Demo** — Workbench with tools (wrench, paintbrush, scissors), each with unique two-hand interaction and haptic signature.

## Complete Game Concepts

### Action / Combat
- **Last Stand** — Wave shooter with a between-wave workbench for physically combining components to build/upgrade weapons. Showcases two-hand interaction, spatial UI, physics, and haptics.

### Puzzle / Stealth
- **The Vault** — Heist puzzle: pick locks by feel, cut wires by hand, crack a safe via haptic clicks. Calm and atmospheric — rare in VR portfolios.

### Platformer / Locomotion
- **Ascent** — Vertical climbing platformer. Use both hands to scale a tower while avoiding hazards. Focuses on locomotion depth and two-hand coordination.

### Horror
- **Séance** — 5-minute atmospheric horror vignette. Investigate a dark room as objects move around you. Demonstrates VR presence design.

## Racing Game Concepts

- **Kart / Arcade Racer** — Low speed ceiling, forgiving physics, bright tracks. Potential gimmicks: portals, gravity flips. *Base: Unity Karting Demo template.*
- **Rally / Off-Road** — Cockpit-based, rough terrain. Strong for haptics (wheel rumble, surface feedback) and physics fidelity.
- **Mech / Hover Racer** — Sci-fi setting with custom control schemes (e.g., hand controllers as throttle/brake levers). Original interaction design.
- **Foot Race / Parkour** — You are the vehicle. Arm-pumping locomotion or parkour movement through a course. Unusual and memorable; directly showcases locomotion design.

### Racing Control Scheme Options
- Grab a physical wheel in-world
- Tilt controllers like a steering wheel
- Hand controllers as independent levers
- Full body lean
